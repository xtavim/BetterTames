# Changelog

## 0.8.0

- Tames lie down to recover instead of dying, and enemies leave them alone while they're down. A butcher knife still kills
- Switch protection per tame with Left Alt + E. Nameplates turn blue when protected and yellow while recovering
- Following tames teleport to you when they fall behind or when you respawn, or on demand with Left Alt + H
- Follow All and Stay All keys, and follow/stay on any tame
- Tames can gain a star from kills, and newborns can be born a star higher
- Tames and creatures being tamed eat from nearby player-built chests
- Taming, breeding, growth and recovery timers in the hover text
- Tames fight wild creatures of their own kind
- Taming time, growth time, love points, heal speed and crowd limit can be set for every tame
- Server settings sync with ServerSync
